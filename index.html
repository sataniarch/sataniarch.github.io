SATANIA ARCHIVE

The Light and The Darkness

Satania Archive is an independent, fan-run collection of Satania's meditations — voice, breath, and silence, addressed at once to the light and to the darkness within. We do not create these recordings. We preserve them.

Every meditation here is a page pulled from the fire of forgetting. We catalogue them by number, like lines in a grimoire, so what was once spoken can be heard again.

The archive is maintained by anonymous keepers from the community. We are not affiliated with the original creator and do not speak on their behalf — we simply guard what could have been lost.

Note

An archive of Satania's meditations. The original channel has been blocked by YouTube. We preserve the legacy. Ave Satanas!
